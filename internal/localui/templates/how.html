How it works

You ask Claude or ChatGPT for classroom work in plain words. It uses CanvasExpert to read and write your Canvas courses. Anything that changes Canvas (scores, new assignments, quizzes) opens a review page first, and nothing posts until you approve it there.

How do I…?

Ask for work
  1. Open Claude or ChatGPT and start a new chat.
  2. Type what you want, the way you'd ask a student teacher. For example: scoring session, draft a quiz on chapter 4, or move Friday's due date to Monday.
  3. Say draft if you only want it saved, not posted.
  4. When a review page opens, read it and choose Post approved, or choose Revise on anything that needs changing.
Change how feedback sounds
  1. Open Settings, Preferences.
  2. Under How feedback should sound, write it the way you'd explain it to a student teacher.
  3. Choose Save feedback voice. It's used the next time work is scored.
Add a note about a student
  1. Open Students and pick the class.
  2. Open Private notes next to the student and type the note.
  3. Choose Save students. Notes are used when scoring and are never shown to the student.
Change which courses show up
  1. Open Courses.
  2. Tick the courses you teach this term and give each a short name.
  3. Choose Save courses.
Update CanvasExpert
  1. In Claude or ChatGPT, type Update CanvasExpert.
  2. It installs the new version. Your courses, notes and settings stay as they are.
Use CanvasExpert on another PC
  1. On this PC, open Settings, Connections and choose a OneDrive or Google Drive folder for your settings.
  2. Install CanvasExpert on the other PC in a regular folder such as Documents, not inside OneDrive.
  3. On the other PC, connect Canvas, then choose the same OneDrive or Google Drive folder in Settings, Connections.
Keep student information private
  1. Turn on Hide student names in Settings, Preferences. Claude or ChatGPT then sees a made-up name for each student in the work it scores.
  2. Never paste your Canvas access token into a chat, an email or a file.
  3. Parent phone numbers and emails on the Students page are never shown to Claude or ChatGPT.
