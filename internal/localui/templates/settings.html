Use CanvasExpert on more than one PC

Your settings, course choices and student notes are saved on this PC. To use them on another PC too:

  1. Choose Browse and pick a folder inside OneDrive or Google Drive, such as OneDrive\CanvasExpert.
  2. Choose Use this folder.
  3. On the other PC, open this page and choose the same folder.

Work you're in the middle of scoring or planning stays on the PC where you started it.
