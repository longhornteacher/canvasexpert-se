# Handoffs

A handoff is bounded execution work for an agent session. It should say whether a change belongs to the Engine or the playbooks. Open work lives in `active/`; completed or superseded handoffs move to `retired/` with a retirement banner. Retired files are history and evidence, not instructions. Retiring one does not mean every observation in it was verified.

Current development authority lives in the root `AGENTS.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md` and `SCORING-RULES.md`; build instructions and dated observations live under `docs/`.

`active/` contains the researched [school-calendar, scoring and parent-update implementation plan](active/SCHOOL-CALENDAR-SCORING-AND-PARENT-UPDATES.md) and its [parent-report contract](active/PARENT-REPORT-CURRENT-TERM.md). They cover automatic school-day lateness preparation and all work due in the past two weeks within the current term, with Due Soon separately. Both are implemented locally in Dev (2026-10-18); live timing writes, packaging and client adoption remain pending. The earlier group-work and parent-update handoffs are in `retired/` with banners, alongside the completed [Studio light/dark handoff](retired/STUDIO-LIGHT-DARK.md), approved reference, implemented screenshots and dated local checks. `retired/` keeps the records that carry unique dated evidence (the fresh-install notes and triage, the New Quiz scoring probe results, the MCP verification record) and the retired Antigravity/streamlined-install spec; everything else is in Git history.
