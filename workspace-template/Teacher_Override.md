# Teacher overrides

This file is yours. It records where you have chosen something different from CE-SE's shipped defaults in `TEACHER.md` and the playbooks. Your agent reads it after those files, and what is written here wins. CE-SE updates never replace it. The playbooks themselves are replaced by each update, so a change you want to keep belongs here and not in a playbook.

You don't need to edit it yourself. When you tell your agent a standing preference, such as "always show me two example comments before posting" or "start every comment with a greeting," or correct it in a way that clearly applies to future work, it adds a line here with the scope, date and a short reason/source. It updates an earlier choice when you change your mind. One-time choices for a single task are not saved here; accepting a draft does not automatically make its style a standing preference.

Setup facts the defaults leave open, such as page style, rubric scale and feedback voice, stay in `context/teaching-profile.md` and `context/grading.md`.

Course-only routines and preferences belong in `context/courses/<course-id>/notes.md`; assignment-only needs and written-response models belong in `context/courses/<course-id>/assignments/<assignment-id>.md`. Your agent reads those at the relevant scope too. See `playbooks/teacher-context.md`. These files follow configured UserSync so another agent or computer can use them.
